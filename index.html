Rosebranch Studio tools

An internal tool of Rosebranch Studio, used only by its owner.

What it is

Rosebranch Studio tools is a private program that runs on the owner's own computer. It helps the owner, Rosebranch Studio (Rafael Ramos Rosa), publish and follow the videos of the studio's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

What it does with Google data

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own channels as private, schedules them, and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions.
  • Reads back the status of those videos to confirm what was uploaded.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's own channels.
  • Sends notification emails to the owner from the owner's Gmail account (send only; it does not read the mailbox).
